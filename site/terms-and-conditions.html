These terms govern orders placed with Occasions Box LLC ("Occasions Box", "we", "us") and use of this website. Placing an order means you accept them. If you are ordering for a company, you confirm you are authorised to bind that company.

1. Orders and acceptance

An order is an offer to buy. It is accepted when we confirm it in writing, or for custom work when both sides approve the final proposal. We may decline an order, and we will tell you why and refund anything already paid.

We try to keep this site accurate, but if an item is listed at an obviously incorrect price we will contact you before charging you rather than fulfil or cancel silently.

2. Pricing, quotes and payment

Shop prices are shown per box. Corporate and event prices are per box or per guest as stated, exclusive of tax, shipping, delivery and any design fee, each of which is quoted with your proposal.

Quotes are valid for 30 days. Custom, corporate and event orders are billed 50% on approval, which is what releases us to buy your products, and the remaining 50% before the order ships or is delivered. Shop orders are paid in full at checkout. Gifting Concierge membership is billed in advance, monthly or annually; boxes sent on a membership bill at their tier price.

We accept card, PayPal and Venmo. A payment counts as received when it clears and we can identify the order it belongs to. A Venmo payment sent directly to @occasionsbox must carry your invoice or proposal number in the note; one we cannot match is held unapplied until you tell us what it is for, and any lead time or ship date runs from the day it is applied.

3. Minimums

Corporate and event gifting starts at 12 boxes. Event favours start at 50 guests. Fully bespoke projects start at $5,000. Gifting Concierge members have no order minimum, which is one of the things a membership buys.

4. Custom and corporate work

For custom work we send a proposal covering contents, packaging, branding and price. Where we produce a proof of printed or branded material, your written approval of that proof is what we produce against, and we are not responsible for an error you approved.

Custom, corporate, event and bespoke orders are final once approved. See Shipping & Returns for how we handle anything that arrives damaged or incorrect.

5. Product availability and substitutions

We buy from small independent makers, and small makers sell out. If an item in a curated box becomes unavailable we substitute one of equal or greater value in keeping with the box, and on custom orders we tell you before we ship. Photographs on this site show representative contents.
