Behind the Box: Meet the Women Behind Occasions Box

Meet the women behind Occasions Box, a New Jersey-based gifting company creating thoughtful custom gifts for clients, teams, events and special occasions.

A beautiful gift may take only a moment to open. Creating one that feels truly personal takes considerably more thought.

That’s the idea behind Occasions Box.

We’re a women-owned, New Jersey-based gifting company, founded in 2017 and built around a simple belief: gifting should feel intentional, personal and beautifully executed.

It Starts With the Recipient

Before choosing what’s inside the box, we think about the person opening it.

Who are they? What are we celebrating? What should this gift communicate? And most importantly, how should they feel when they receive it?

Those questions guide everything that follows.

Then Comes the Curation

Products. Colors. Textures. Packaging. Ribbon. Branding. Personalized notes.

Every element is considered individually, but designed to work together.

Whether we’re creating client appreciation gifts, employee holiday boxes, event gifting or something for a meaningful personal milestone, our goal is always the same: make the recipient feel genuinely thought of.

Women-Owned. New Jersey-Based. Personally Curated.

Occasions Box was created in 2017 by women who appreciate the details, the ones that turn a collection of beautiful things into a memorable gifting experience.

We’re not interested in making gifting feel mass-produced.

We want it to feel like you thought of everything, even when we handled everything for you.

That’s the beauty of gifting with Occasions Box.

Thoughtfully curated. Beautifully presented. Seamlessly executed.

Ready to create something special? Start your custom gifting project with Occasions Box.
