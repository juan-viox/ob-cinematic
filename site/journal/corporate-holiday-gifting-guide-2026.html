The 2026 Corporate Holiday Gifting Guide

Make holiday gifting effortless: curated corporate gifts for clients, employees and teams, with custom branding, packaging and delivery by Occasions Box.

The best corporate gifts do more than check a name off a holiday list. They make clients feel appreciated, employees feel valued and important relationships feel remembered.

At Occasions Box, we believe corporate gifting should feel personal, beautifully presented and effortless for the person sending it.

Gifts for Clients

Client gifts are an opportunity to say thank you in a way that feels genuine. Consider elevated food and beverage pairings, beautifully curated lifestyle pieces, personalized keepsakes or custom gift boxes designed around your brand and recipient.

The goal isn’t simply to send something. It’s to create an experience they’ll remember.

Gifts for Employees and Teams

Holiday gifting is also an opportunity to recognize the people behind your business.

From individual appreciation gifts to company-wide gifting programs, thoughtful details such as personalized notes, custom packaging and carefully selected products can transform a simple holiday gift into something meaningful.

VIP and Executive Gifting

For your most important relationships, a more elevated approach may be appropriate. Premium products, sophisticated packaging and thoughtful personalization create a gift that feels distinctive without being excessive.

Branded, Without Feeling Promotional

Corporate gifting doesn’t have to look like promotional merchandise.

Subtle branding can create a polished brand experience while keeping the gift itself at the center: custom ribbon, packaging, gift tags, cards or thoughtfully incorporated company colors.

Holiday Gifting Without the Holiday Stress

From concept and sourcing to customization, packaging and delivery, Occasions Box handles the details from start to finish.

Simply tell us who you’re gifting, your approximate budget, quantity and what you’d like the experience to convey. We’ll take it from there.

Now booking for 2026

Corporate holiday orders run a 3–4 week lead time from approval between mid-October and the end of December, when our makers and carriers are at their busiest, plus 1–7 business days in transit. Custom sourcing and branded packaging are the two things that benefit most from extra time, so the earlier a project starts, the more open the options stay.
