How It Works

Four Steps, Start to Doorstep

  1. Step 01

    Tell Us About Your Recipient

    Share the occasion, preferences, and budget. Whether it's one box or a hundred, our concierge team starts with understanding exactly who you're gifting and why.

  2. Step 02

    We Curate the Perfect Box

    Our team handpicks premium artisan products, matched to the recipient's taste and the tone of the occasion: candles, gourmet treats, skincare, stationery.

  3. Step 03

    Beautifully Wrapped & Prepared

    Every item is arranged in our signature gift box with tissue, ribbon, and a handwritten note. Opening it should feel like an occasion in itself.

  4. Step 04

    Delight, Delivered

    We ship nationwide. What arrives does not look like it came out of a warehouse, because it did not.
