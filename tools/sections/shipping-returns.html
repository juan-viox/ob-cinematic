Order processing

Ready-to-gift boxes from the shop are packed and handed to the carrier within 1–3 business days. Orders placed on a weekend or a federal holiday begin processing the next business day.

Gifting Concierge sends are curated and dispatched on your membership's turnaround, not the custom lead time below.

Lead time on custom, corporate and event orders

Plan on a 2–4 week lead time from the day your order is approved, and 3–4 weeks for orders shipping between 15 October and 31 December, when our makers and carriers are at their busiest. Transit is on top of that: allow a further 1–7 business days once the order leaves us, depending on the destination.

The clock starts when three things are done, not before: the proposal is approved in writing, the 50% deposit has cleared, and everything we need from you is final — artwork, branded materials, the note copy and the complete recipient list. If any of those arrive later, the ship date moves by the same number of days, and we will tell you the new one.

Once those are in hand we confirm your ship date in writing and we hold it. If a sourcing delay puts it at risk we tell you as soon as we know, not after the date has passed, and we substitute an item of equal or greater value rather than miss your date. Working inside the standard lead time is often possible and is quoted as a rush.

If you are gifting for a dated event, tell us the date at the start. We would rather turn work away than accept a timeline we are not confident we can meet.

Payment terms

Shop orders are paid in full at checkout. Custom, corporate and event orders are billed 50% on approval and 50% before the order ships or is delivered.

Shipping methods and cost

We ship nationwide. Ground shipping is our standard service and is included on Gifting Concierge memberships. On shop orders, shipping is calculated at checkout. On corporate, event and bespoke orders, shipping is quoted with your proposal, because cost depends on quantity, destination and how the boxes are packed.

Rush production and expedited delivery are available on request and are billed in addition.

Local hand delivery

We hand deliver and set up ourselves within Bergen County and a 25 mile radius of Fort Lee, New Jersey. Hand delivery beyond that radius is available and is quoted with your proposal.

Delivery addresses

Please give us complete and accurate addresses, including any suite, floor or mail room detail. We ship to the address supplied on the order and we cannot be responsible for a gift delivered to an address that was entered incorrectly.

Once an order has shipped we cannot reroute it. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable we will contact you and can send it again once the address is corrected; reshipment is billed at cost.

On multi-recipient corporate sends, you are responsible for the accuracy of the recipient list you provide. We will flag addresses that fail validation before we ship.

Damaged, lost or incorrect

Every box is packed by hand to travel, but carriers are carriers. If a gift arrives damaged, if something is missing, or if the wrong item was sent, email Hello@occasionsbox.com within 5 business days of delivery with photographs of the box and its contents. We will replace the affected items or the whole gift at our cost.

If tracking shows a package as delivered but the recipient does not have it, tell us within 5 business days and we will open a claim with the carrier and work it through with you.

Returns

All sales are final. Because every box is curated, hand-packed and often personalised for a named recipient, we do not accept returns or exchanges, and this applies without exception to custom, corporate, event and bespoke orders.

That is not a policy for hiding behind. Anything that arrives damaged, defective or not what we agreed is ours to put right, and the section above is how we do it.

Cancellations and changes

Shop orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship. Once a box has shipped, the section above applies.

Custom, corporate and event orders can be changed or cancelled without charge up until we approve your final proposal and begin sourcing. After that point, products have been bought and materials have been printed on your behalf, so cancellation refunds any amount not yet committed to your order and design fees already earned are non-refundable.

Perishable and seasonal items

Some boxes contain chocolate, confections or other items that do not travel well in extreme heat. In warm months we may adjust packing, hold a shipment for a better delivery window, or substitute an item of equal or greater value rather than send something that will not arrive in the condition we intend.
