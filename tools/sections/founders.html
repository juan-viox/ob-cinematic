Meet the Founders

Kari Aragon
Co-Founder

Twenty years in the legal field taught Kari that the details are the whole job. At Occasions Box she runs operations, logistics and client experience — the recipient lists, the ship dates, the hundred small confirmations a client never sees. She would rather tell you a date is not possible than promise one she cannot hold.

Sarah De Jesus
Co-Founder

Sarah spent a decade designing weddings and luxury events as founder of Dreamers Joy Events & Design. She leads the creative side of Occasions Box: what goes in the box, and how it feels when the lid comes off. She sources from small independent makers in small batches, and builds every box backwards from one question — what will the person opening it actually keep?
