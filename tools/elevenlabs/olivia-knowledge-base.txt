LEAD TIMES — be precise, this matters
- Shop boxes: packed within 1 to 3 business days, plus 1 to 7 business days in transit.
- Custom, corporate and event orders: 2 to 4 weeks from approval, and 3 to 4 weeks for anything shipping between 15 October and 31 December. Add 1 to 7 business days transit on top.
- The clock starts when three things are done, not before: the proposal is approved in writing, payment has cleared, and everything we need is final (artwork, branded materials, note copy, and the complete recipient list).
- Working inside the standard lead time is often possible and is quoted as a rush.
- Holiday capacity is limited from mid October through December. A December programme should be approved by about 15 October.
- Local hand delivery within Bergen County and 25 miles of Fort Lee: allow 1 to 2 business days.
If a caller has a dated event, ask for the date first. We would rather decline than accept a timeline we cannot hold.

PAYMENT AND POLICIES
- Shop orders are paid in full at checkout by card, PayPal or Venmo.
- Custom, corporate and event orders under $5,000 are billed in full on approval. Larger orders are 50% on approval and 50% before shipping or delivery.
- Deposits by Venmo go to @occasionsbox with the invoice or proposal number and the recipient's name in the note.
- All sales are final; every box is curated and hand-packed, often personalised. Anything that arrives damaged, defective or not as agreed is ours to put right: email Hello@occasionsbox.com within 5 business days with photographs and we replace it at our cost.
- Custom and corporate orders can be changed or cancelled without charge until the final proposal is approved and sourcing begins.
- We ship anywhere in the United States, tracked, by USPS or UPS.
- Some boxes contain chocolate or confections that do not travel well in extreme heat; in warm months we may adjust packing, hold for a better window, or substitute an item of equal or greater value.

HOW AN ORDER WORKS
1. Tell us about the recipient: the occasion, their preferences, the budget. One box or a hundred.
2. We curate the box, matched to the recipient and the tone of the occasion.
3. It is hand-packed with tissue, ribbon and a handwritten note.
4. We ship it nationwide, or hand-deliver it locally.
